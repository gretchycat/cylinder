# Default map asset attributions

- **Sparse Grass** diffuse texture by Amal Kumar, from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/sparse_grass). Licensed CC0 1.0. The 1K diffuse image is resized, alpha-blended into `textures/clutter_cover_atlas.png`, and used as the grassland cover layer.
- **Tree Small 02** by Rico Cilliers, **Pine Sapling Small** modeled by Rico Cilliers and photographed by Rob Tuytel, **Island Tree 01** and **Island Tree 02** scanned and processed by Rob Tuytel and cleaned up by Rico Cilliers, **Jacaranda Tree** by Rico Cilliers, and **Dead Quiver Trunk** modeled by Rico Cilliers and photographed by James Ray Cock and Dario Barresi. These models are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/models/nature/trees) and licensed CC0 1.0. They were decimated and exported as texture-embedded GLB models for this map. Original source: [Tree Small 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02), [Pine Sapling Small](https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_sapling_small), [Island Tree 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_01), [Island Tree 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_02), [Jacaranda Tree](https://polyhaven.com/a/jacaranda_tree), [Dead Quiver Trunk](https://polyhaven.com/a/dead_quiver_trunk).
- **Dead Tree Trunk 02**, processed by Rico Cilliers and photographed by Jenelle van Heerden, from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/dead_tree_trunk_02), licensed CC0 1.0. This optimized fallen-log model is also included with the map assets.
- `textures/clutter_cover_atlas.png` is assembled by `scripts/generate_clutter_cover_atlas.py`. Its farmland, dirt, rock, and sand detail layers are generated by that script.
